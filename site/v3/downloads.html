DOWNLOAD ARCHITECTURE

One interface.
Swappable delivery.

The website's download layer is deliberately separated from its presentation. A provider can change later without forcing a redesign of the download UI. Until an independent provider is configured and validated, the official GitHub release remains the concrete destination.
