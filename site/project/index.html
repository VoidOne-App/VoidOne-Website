PROJECT / WHY IT EXISTS

START WITH
THE PLAYER.

VoidOne is an attempt to build a native PC gaming platform around the player rather than around the storefront. That means local control, useful system context, inspectable engineering and no ad-driven layer.

PROJECT ATLAS / PLAYER · LOCAL · EVIDENCE · SOURCEACTIVE DEVELOPMENT
01 / PRODUCT

Not a storefront

VoidOne is a platform layer around games you already use. It does not exist to become another store competing for your attention.

02 / ENGINEERING

Native by choice

The current foundation is C++23 with Qt/QML, SQLite, CMake/Ninja and CTest, targeting native desktop integration.

03 / CONTROL

Local first

Important player state and workflows should remain locally controlled whenever practical.

04 / TRUTH

Evidence over marketing

Implemented, experimental and planned capabilities are intentionally separated.
