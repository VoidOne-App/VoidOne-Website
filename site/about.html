ABOUT VOIDONE

Games first.
Not stores.

VoidOne is an open-source native PC gaming platform being engineered around the idea that the player's games, hardware, data and workflows should remain at the center.

01

Native First

Prefer native technologies and operating-system capabilities when they improve performance, integration or reliability.

02

Privacy by Design

Player information should not be collected, transmitted or monetized without a legitimate technical reason.

03

Local First

Whenever practical, important player data remains locally controlled.

04

Evidence Over Marketing

Technical claims should be backed by implementation, testing, benchmarks, documentation or reproducible evidence.

THE PRINCIPLE

Stable is earned.

VoidOne is intentionally experimental today. Stable is treated as an engineering milestone reached through reliability, testing, security validation, performance work and complete release documentation.
